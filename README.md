A tiny paper-trading bot that holds all seven Solana coins in equal slices, like buy-and-hold, keeping about 0.5% in USDC. Whenever a slice drifts more than 0.45% of equity away from its equal share, the bot trades it back, selling what just ran ahead of the basket and buying what lagged behind. That rebalancing captures the back-and-forth between the coins, a small edge over buy-and-hold that does not depend on whether the market rises or falls. A trade-count guard narrows the band in a calm market so the entry always meets the 8-trade guardrail.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
